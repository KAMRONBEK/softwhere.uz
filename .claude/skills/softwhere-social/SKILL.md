# SoftWhere social

SoftWhere sells mobile apps with AI built in to buyers in the US and Europe. Most first clients will come through the founders' own networks, so posts come from a person (usually the US co-founder or the Tashkent founder), not from a logo. The goal of every post is to make a potential client think "these people know what they're doing and I could talk to them".

## Who we're talking to

- Founders and product leads who need a mobile app or AI features
- Islamic fintech and Muslim-market product teams (halal investing, home finance, takaful, giving, Quran learning)
- Logistics, mobility and field-operations companies
- US agencies looking for a white-label development partner

## What to post about

- **Building:** a real problem from current work and how the team solved it (no client names without permission)
- **AI inside apps:** what actually works, what doesn't, costs and trade-offs
- **Halal finance products:** building contract flows correctly, UX without interest language, working with Shariah boards
- **The two-time-zone team:** how the California–Tashkent handoff works day to day
- **Lessons and numbers:** estimates, timelines, what an MVP really costs
- **Offers, sparingly:** the Blueprint, the AI Feature Pilot, the App Rescue Audit. At most one post in five sells something.

## Voice

First person, plain and specific, like explaining something to a smart friend. Short paragraphs. One idea per post. No hype words, no engagement bait ("Agree?", "Comment YES"), no walls of emoji or hashtags (zero to three hashtags). Never invent numbers, clients or results; use [brackets] for missing facts. Past work done while employed elsewhere is "our engineers have shipped", never "our client".

If `references/voice-samples.md` contains real posts from the person posting, match their rhythm and word choice. If it's empty, write in the voice above and suggest the user add samples.

## Formats

**LinkedIn post:** a first line that stands alone and makes people click "see more"; three to six short paragraphs; one concrete takeaway; a soft close (a question or "DM me if you're building X"). Under 1,300 characters unless the story needs more.

**X post or thread:** the first post must work alone; threads of three to six posts; each post one idea.

**Short video (15–45 seconds, vertical 9:16):**
- A hook in the first two seconds, spoken and shown as on-screen text
- A shot list: what the camera or screen shows, second by second
- On-screen captions for every spoken line (most people watch muted)
- Real faces or real product screens. No AI avatars; buyers trust real people.
- Default to no background music, since the founders prefer to avoid it. Use voice, natural sound and captions. Confirm with the user if unsure.
- End on one clear action and the web address.

**Carousel:** six to eight slides, one sentence each, the last slide the action.

**Content calendar:** a table of date, platform, person posting, topic, format and a one-line hook. Three posts a week is a sustainable default.
