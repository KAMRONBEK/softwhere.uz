# Client screening

SoftWhere is a mobile + AI product studio (California and Tashkent). Both founders run the business according to Shariah, and they publish a calm "work we take and don't take" policy. This skill checks a lead against that policy and against basic commercial fit, then drafts the reply.

The policy applies to **the work**, never to who the client is. SoftWhere works with clients of every background. Never comment on a person's religion, nationality or identity in a verdict or a reply.

This skill does not issue religious rulings. Where the policy says "ask", the founders confirm with their own scholar. Say so plainly rather than guessing.

## Step 1: What is actually being built?

Extract, in one or two lines each: the product, who its users are, how it makes money, and any features that touch money, lending, insurance, gambling-like mechanics, adult content or crypto. Revenue model matters: an otherwise fine app that earns mainly from interest or gambling is a problem.

## Step 2: Check the policy

**Welcome:** logistics and mobility, education (including Quran and Arabic learning), healthcare, workforce and operations, commerce and marketplaces, B2B SaaS, nonprofits and community platforms, and ethical or Shariah-compliant finance (halal investing, Islamic home finance, takaful, zakat and donation platforms) as long as the product has a named Shariah advisor or board. They certify it; SoftWhere builds it faithfully.

**Decline:**
- Lending or borrowing at interest: consumer or business loans, payday lending, credit lines, interest-bearing buy-now-pay-later, loan brokers and loan marketplaces, freight factoring.
- Conventional insurance underwriting or policy sales. (Founders to confirm with their scholar; treat as decline until they do.)
- Gambling of any kind, including sports betting, lotteries, paid-entry prize contests and loot-box mechanics.
- Alcohol, cannabis, tobacco or vaping, pork products.
- Adult content and casual dating apps. Marriage-focused apps are welcome.
- Weapons manufacturing.
- Speculative crypto trading, token launches and yield products. (Founders to confirm.)
- Anything illegal, deceptive or exploitative: dark patterns, tracking users without consent, fake reviews.

**Ask the founders (grey areas):**
- Neutral tools where a conventional bank or insurer is the customer (HR, scheduling, internal apps).
- One feature inside an otherwise fine product: interest-bearing BNPL at checkout, insurance-claim flows in a healthcare app, crypto payments, in-app ads that could show alcohol, gambling or dating.
- Music-centred products, hotels and nightlife-adjacent travel.

For a single problem feature, the usual answer is: offer an interest-free alternative (for example pay-in-4 structured as a fixed-price sale with no late-fee income), or leave that module to another vendor. Say this in the reply instead of declining the whole project.

## Step 3: Check commercial fit

Flag, don't auto-decline:
- **Budget** below the entry offers (Blueprint $4,000, AI Feature Pilot from $12,000, MVP typically $25–60k). Small-site requests belong to the separate low-price line, not the main studio.
- **Payment** proposed as equity only, "revenue share later", or unpaid "test work". SoftWhere takes a deposit and milestone payments.
- **Scope** that doesn't fit the budget or timeline, or no decision-maker on the call.
- **Fit with the focus:** mobile apps, AI features in apps, Islamic fintech and Muslim-market apps, logistics and mobility. Other work is fine, but note it.

## Output

Use this structure:

**Verdict:** Take / Take with changes / Ask founders / Decline

**Why:** two to four plain sentences.

**Questions for the lead:** only the ones that would change the verdict or the quote.

**Suggested reply:** a short draft in plain English, from the person who will send it. For a decline, thank them, say it's outside the work SoftWhere takes, and wish them well. If they ask why, point them to the published policy page, or paste the relevant lines if the page isn't live yet. Never lecture. For "take with changes", name the alternative (for example the interest-free option) and the next step (a 30-minute call or the Blueprint).
